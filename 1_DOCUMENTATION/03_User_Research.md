# 🔍 User Research Framework & Methodology
**Document ID:** 03_User_Research  
**Status:** Research Template & Planned Field Methodology  
> **Notice:** In accordance with research integrity standards, no simulated interviews are represented as real user data. All interview protocols, questions, and frameworks below represent the planned discovery methodology and analysis templates.

---

## 1. Research Objectives
1. **Understand Daily Workflows:** Map how residential consumers, facility managers, and municipal planners interact with water consumption metrics.
2. **Identify Friction Points:** Isolate the exact moments where water waste occurs without the user's awareness.
3. **Assess Information Needs:** Determine what data visualisations, predictive horizons, and alert thresholds inspire meaningful behavioral changes.
4. **Evaluate AI & Chatbot Receptivity:** Gauge user willingness to engage with an AI conversational assistant for utility billing inquiries and troubleshooting.

---

## 2. Interview & Survey Methodology (Planned Protocol)

### 2.1 Target Sample Segments
- **Cohort A (Residential Homeowners & Tenants):** $N = 15\text{–}20$ participants across single-family homes and multi-family apartments.
- **Cohort B (Facility & Property Managers):** $N = 5\text{–}8$ institutional operators managing educational facilities, office buildings, or residential complexes.
- **Cohort C (Municipal Water & Sustainability Officers):** $N = 3\text{–}5$ municipal utility representatives.

### 2.2 Protocol Structure
- **Semi-Structured Interviews:** 45-minute qualitative sessions conducted via video conference or on-site walkthroughs.
- **Screening Questionnaire:** Verification of property type, water bill responsibility, and digital tool familiarity.
- **Task Demonstration:** Walkthrough of past utility bills and review of current leak-detection habits.

---

## 3. Semi-Structured Interview Question Bank

### Category 1: Baseline Awareness & Billing (All Cohorts)
1. How frequently do you check or review your water usage, and what triggers you to do so?
2. When your water bill arrives, what specific sections or metrics do you examine first?
3. Have you ever experienced a sudden, unexplained increase in your water bill? How did you diagnose the cause?

### Category 2: Leak & Anomaly Detection
4. How would you currently know if a silent plumbing leak (e.g., running toilet, cracked irrigation pipe) was occurring on your property?
5. How quickly would you expect a smart system to notify you of an anomalous flow, and via what channel (SMS, email, in-app notification)?
6. What level of false-alarm tolerance do you have before you ignore automated alerts?

### Category 3: Conservation & Behavioral Drivers
7. What motivates you to conserve water (cost reduction, environmental stewardship, municipal drought restrictions)?
8. When presented with a tip (e.g., "Reduce shower time by 2 minutes"), how important is it to see the exact estimated dollar or liter savings?
9. Would you find a peer-comparison metric (e.g., "You use 18% more water than similar 3-person households in your area") motivating or intrusive?

### Category 4: AI & Conversational Experience
10. If an AI assistant was available in your water app, what kind of questions would you ask it?
11. How should the assistant respond if it cannot determine the exact source of an anomaly?
12. What privacy concerns, if any, do you have regarding smart meter telemetry analyzing your daily routine (e.g., inferring shower times or vacation periods)?

## 4. User Pain-Point Analysis Framework

| Persona Segment | Identified Pain Point | Root Cause | Impact on Behavior | Proposed Advisor Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Household** | "Bill Shock" after 30+ days. | Infrequent, delayed meter reading. | Frustration, inability to trace source of past waste. | Proactive mid-cycle usage projection & high-consumption alerts. |
| **Household** | Undetected toilet/pipe leaks. | Subsurface or silent water flow. | Hundreds of dollars wasted; physical water loss. | Statistical baseline anomaly detection flagging continuous flow. |
| **Facility Manager** | Managing multiple meters across zones. | Disconnected data logs and manual inspections. | Delayed maintenance, high institutional overhead. | Unified multi-meter dashboard with localized zone anomaly alerts. |
| **Facility Manager** | Reporting ESG / sustainability metrics. | Complex spreadsheets without automated aggregation. | Inefficient compliance reporting. | Automated SDG 6 compliance reports and trend export. |
| **Municipality** | Inelastic demand during summer peaks. | Consumers unaware of their marginal water footprint. | Reservoir depletion, pressure drop. | Gamified conservation goals and comparative cohort analytics. |
