## Case Study 1: East Bay Municipal Utility District (EBMUD) Smart Meter Pilot
- **Location:** San Francisco Bay Area, California, USA
- **Challenge:** Severe recurring California drought conditions requiring voluntary residential water reduction.
- **Intervention:** Automated smart meter feedback portal providing 10,000 households with bi-weekly usage reports, leak alerts, and neighbor comparisons.
- **Observed Results:**
  - Households receiving smart feedback reduced total water consumption by **5.0% to 6.6%**.
  - Households identified with continuous toilet flapper leaks saved an average of **360 liters/day** once alerted.
  - Overall customer satisfaction with utility communication rose by 22%.
- **Project Relevance:** Proves that non-intrusive leak alerting and comparative consumption feedback reliably triggers consumer behavioral change.

## Case Study 2: Singapore PUB Smart Water Meter Programme
- **Location:** Singapore (Public Utilities Board)
- **Challenge:** Land-scarce city-state with dense multi-family residential high-rises and zero natural aquifer reserves.
- **Intervention:** Deployment of 300,000 digital smart meters paired with a mobile consumer portal detecting nocturnal leaks and disaggregating domestic appliance consumption.
- **Observed Results:**
  - Early detection of internal leaks within households accounted for **over 70% of detected water loss recovery**.
  - Participating households demonstrated an aggregate **10% to 15% reduction** in domestic consumption within 6 months.
- **Project Relevance:** Demonstrates that urban multi-family residential populations benefit significantly from disaggregated fixture feedback.

## Case Study 3: University Campus Multi-Building Conservation
- **Location:** University of Arizona Water Resources Research Center
- **Challenge:** Substantial water expenditures across dormitories, dining halls, and academic lab buildings without zone-level sub-meter visibility.
- **Intervention:** Installation of sub-meters combined with a facilities dashboard flagging after-hours water flow and cooling tower blow-down anomalies.
- **Observed Results:**
  - Uncovered a silent underground cooling loop leak losing 42,000 liters/day in an engineering hall.
  - Overall campus potable water demand dropped by **24%** following corrective actions.
- **Project Relevance:** Directly validates **Persona 2 (Institutional Facility Manager)** workflows and alert escalation tiers.
